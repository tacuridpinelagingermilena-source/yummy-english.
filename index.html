04 · Keep learning

Why learn food vocabulary? 🥗

Food words are useful in everyday life. They help us talk about our favorite meals, order food at a restaurant, go shopping, and communicate with other people. Every new word is a new opportunity to learn!

Remember: Practice a little every day. You can do it!
